# User Guide — NewsFeed

Welcome to **NewsFeed** — a personalised global news app that delivers the latest headlines in a fast, swipeable format. This guide walks you through everything you can do, step by step.

---

## What Is NewsFeed?

NewsFeed automatically collects news from 150+ sources around the world — newspapers, TV channels, wire agencies, and tech publications — translates everything into English, and shows it to you one story at a time in a vertical swipe format, just like TikTok but for news.

You choose your **region** (e.g. Belgium, USA, UAE, Mexico) and your **topics** (e.g. Tech, Sports, Politics), and the app curates a personalised feed refreshed every day.

---

## Using the Web App

### Opening the App

Open the app in your browser. You will land directly on your **news feed**.

---

### The Feed Screen

The main screen shows one full-screen news card at a time.

**How to navigate:**
- **Scroll down** (mouse wheel or trackpad) to move to the next story
- **Scroll up** to go back to the previous story
- Each card snaps perfectly into place — no partial cards

**What you see on each card:**
- A background photo from the article
- The **headline** at the bottom
- The **source name** (e.g. "BBC", "Le Soir", "Al Jazeera") and **topic tag** (e.g. "Politics")
- A **one-sentence summary** below the headline

**Reading the full article:**
- Swipe or drag the card **upward from the bottom** (or click/tap the summary area) to open the full story
- A drawer slides up showing the complete AI-translated article text
- Swipe it back down to return to the card

**End of feed:**
- When you reach the last story, a "You're all caught up!" screen appears
- Click **Back to Top** to return to the first card

---

### The Discover Screen

Tap the **Discover** icon (compass) in the bottom navigation bar to customise your feed.

#### Choosing Your Region

Regions are grouped by continent:

| Group | Available regions |
|---|---|
| 🇪🇺 Europe | Belgium, Netherlands, France, Germany, South Europe, North Europe, Europe |
| 🌍 Middle East | UAE, Middle East |
| 🌏 Asia | India, China, Asia |
| 🌎 Americas | USA, Canada, Mexico, South America, Brazil |
| 🌍 Africa | Africa |
| 🌏 Oceania | Oceania |
| 🌐 Global | World |

- Tap any region pill to select it (highlighted in red when active)
- **Tip:** Selecting a country (e.g. Belgium) also includes pan-regional sources like Euronews. Selecting "Europe" shows all European country feeds combined.

#### Choosing Your Topics

Below the region selector, choose one or more **topic chips**:

`Trending` `Politics` `Business` `Tech` `Science` `Health` `Sports` `Entertainment`

- Tap a chip to add it to your filter (it turns purple when selected)
- Tap it again to remove it
- **Trending** is a special mode — it shows recent stories sorted by recency regardless of topic
- You can select multiple topics at once (e.g. Tech + Science)

#### Applying Your Choices

Scroll to the bottom and tap **Apply & Browse Feed**. Your choices are saved automatically and will be remembered the next time you open the app.

## Using the Mobile App (iOS / Android)

### How to View the App on Your Phone

The mobile app is built with **Expo Go** — a free app that lets you run it directly on your device without installing anything from the App Store.

**Steps:**

1. Download **Expo Go** from the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android)
2. Open Expo Go on your phone
3. Ask the person who shared this app with you for the **QR code** or the **Expo link** (it looks like `exp://...`)
4. On iPhone: open the Camera app, point it at the QR code, and tap the banner that appears
5. On Android: inside Expo Go, tap "Scan QR Code" and scan it
6. The app loads directly on your phone — no installation needed

> **Note:** Your phone and the computer running the app must be connected to the same Wi-Fi network, OR the app must be published to a public URL.

---

### Feed Tab (first tab)

The bottom of the screen has two tabs:
- **Feed** (home icon) — your personalised news scroll
- **Discover** (compass icon) — your filter settings

On the **Feed tab**:
- **Swipe up** to go to the next story
- **Swipe down** to go back
- Each story fills your entire screen with a photo background and the headline at the bottom
- Tap the **summary text** at the bottom to expand and read the full article
- When you reach the end, tap **Refresh** to reload with the latest news

---

### Discover Tab (second tab)

Works exactly the same as the web version:

1. **Pick your region** — tap any region button. A red checkmark confirms your selection.
2. **Pick your topics** — tap chips to select/deselect. Active topics appear in purple.
3. Tap **Apply & Browse Feed** at the bottom — this returns you to the feed with your new settings applied.

Your preferences are saved to your device and remembered across app restarts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How fresh is the news?**
The feed refreshes automatically every day at 4:00 AM UTC. Articles older than 12 hours are removed. When the server restarts, it also fetches the latest news immediately.

**Why is everything in English?**
Articles from non-English sources (Dutch, French, German, Arabic, Spanish, etc.) are automatically translated to English by AI before being displayed.

**What does "Trending" mean?**
"Trending" shows the most recently published articles from your selected region, sorted newest first, regardless of topic category.

**Why are there no articles for my region?**
Try selecting a broader region (e.g. "Europe" instead of "Germany") or clearing your topic filters. New articles appear after the next scheduled refresh.

**Can I read the original article?**
The app displays AI-translated and summarised content. The original source name is shown on every card so you can search for the original if needed.

**Does the app work offline?**
No. The app requires an internet connection to load news from the server.
